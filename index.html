Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with TravelCare? ▼

Getting started is easy! Download the TravelCare app from the iOS App Store or Google Play Store, create your account, complete the verification process, and you're ready to start working or posting jobs.

Is TravelCare available in my country? ▼

TravelCare is available in 7 major global regions and supports 12 languages and 10 currencies. We're continuously expanding to new regions. Check the app to see if your country is currently supported.

How are payments handled? ▼

Payments are secured through escrow protection. When a job is completed, the payment is held securely until both parties confirm completion. We support multiple payment methods and currencies.

What is the emergency support feature? ▼

Our emergency support feature provides one-tap access to emergency services with real 911 integration. Your location and profile information are automatically shared with emergency responders for faster assistance.

How does verification work? ▼

Verification includes background checks, certification verification, and identity confirmation. This ensures all professionals on the platform are trustworthy and qualified. The process typically takes 2-3 business days.

What are the fees? ▼

TravelCare takes a 5% commission on completed jobs. All job listings have equal visibility - no one has to pay extra to be seen. Professionals keep 100% of their earnings after the commission.
